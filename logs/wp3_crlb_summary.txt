Interpretation:
  - Static mode (pre-Kalman) is the 0.5-bug regime: without 5km baseline,
    initial fused estimate has RMSE > 0.2m → anchor saturates → win_rate=0.5.

    even with small baseline, but ONLY if the initial anchor didn't already
    lock the tracker onto a degenerate solution (the actual 0.5-bug mechanism).
